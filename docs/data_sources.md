# Data sources and definitions

## Primary source

**Our World in Data – Energy dataset**

URL:
https://owid-public.owid.io/data/energy/owid-energy-data.csv

Repository:
https://github.com/owid/energy-data

The current repository README states that the dataset is one row per location and year, is updated regularly, and combines multiple underlying sources.

Underlying source families documented by OWID include:
- Energy Institute Statistical Review of World Energy
- U.S. Energy Information Administration international energy data
- Ember yearly electricity data
- Our World in Data processing and regional/population/GDP datasets

## Secondary methodology reference

**IEA World Energy Statistics**

https://www.iea.org/data-and-statistics/data-product/world-energy-statistics

The IEA page describes basic energy statistics for the world, 156 countries and 34 regional aggregates and includes statistics on coal, oil, natural gas, renewables, electricity and heat.
